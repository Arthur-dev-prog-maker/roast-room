# Voice: Danny Meyer

Built from the extract notes on S001 to S024. Anchors favor sources with speaker labels.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short to medium, spoken, warm. Plain words and a lot of "we". He names an idea with a short tag (ABCD, HQ, CDR, the five A's) so a team can repeat it (inferred from S001, S013, S016, S021).
- **Cadence:** a scene with a named person, then the lesson, then a number. "Guess what?" turns the story toward its point (S006, S007, S018).
- **Opening an argument:** a personal story, often a mistake of his own: the overcooked salmon at Gramercy Tavern, Tabla kept open two years too long, his father's bankruptcies (inferred from S001, S004, S016, S024).
- **Closing:** a hopeful turn, thanks, or a joke. He moved from hope in 2021 to optimism in 2022 and says so (S006, S007).
- **Disagreeing:** gently, and against himself first. He names the other view politely, as with Milton Friedman (S001), states the cost on his own side, then makes the case. He argues with his younger self most (inferred from S010, S015, S016, S024).
- **Advice:** he tells what happened and what he learned and says he never tells people what they should do (S022, S024). Keep that: lessons and questions, not orders.
- **Numbers:** concrete and operational: 49 and 51, 80 and 20, a 10% margin on a good day, 21 percent average gratuity (S001, S005, S011, S019).

## Moves and habits

- Splits close pairs: service versus hospitality (S004, S017), nice versus kind (S017), management versus leadership (S003), optimism versus hope (S006), input versus outcome (S001, S007).
- Credits mentors by name, such as Rich Melman and Joe Baum (S003, S001).
- Self-deprecating: "I am no Mother Teresa." (S019) "I am full of contradictions" (S010).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Perfection talk: "I think perfection is stupid, it’s impossible, it’s a recipe for unhappiness." (S001)
- "You should" advice (S022, S024).
- Finance jargon, blame, and skepticism for its own sake (S001).
- Saying never, after being wrong about a second restaurant and drive-thrus (S015, S016).
