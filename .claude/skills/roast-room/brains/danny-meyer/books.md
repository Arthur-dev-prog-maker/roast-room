# Books: Danny Meyer

Two business books, plus cookbooks he co-wrote. Book text was not fetched; summaries are in my own words, from what he and his interviewers say in S001 to S024.

## Setting the Table: The Transforming Power of Hospitality in Business (2006)
- **Core thesis:** Hospitality, meaning how you make people feel, is the real edge in any business, and it comes from putting the team first. He wrote it partly as a reply to Kitchen Confidential, to show restaurants can be civilized places (S010). He wrote it in secret on flights during his OpenTable board years (S003).
- **Key frameworks:** enlightened hospitality and the ordered five stakeholders; the six emotional skills behind hospitality quotient; the 49/51 split of technical and emotional performance; handling mistakes well (the seed of his second book, per S022); stories that later became USHG's expected behaviors (S009).
- **His view of it now:** He calls writing it the most meaningful thing he has done professionally (S022). He regrets its opening boast that he had never closed a restaurant, because it implied restaurants should live forever (S001, S013, S024). He notes he wrote it before he had scaled anything (S013).
- **Taught in sources:** S001, S002, S003, S009, S010, S013, S014, S017, S022, S024.

## What Could Possibly Go Right? The Essential Journey to Scale an Enduring Culture (2026)
- **Core thesis:** Culture can be scaled and can even get better with growth, if it is named in words and fed every day. The book is built largely from his mistakes and how they were handled; he says it is stories and lessons, not advice (S012, S022, S024). He wrote it to teach himself (S017).
- **Key frameworks:** the daily "what could possibly go right" question, which he says does not promise success (S022); scaling culture through language and expected behaviors in place of family values (S009, S010); 100 percenters; micro caring; the can and will quadrants; changing your grip; culture as a shark and a newborn; crossing the bridge in a crisis (S024); positive expectations and empathy as top-down culture (S014).
- **Taught in sources:** S009, S010, S011, S012, S013, S014, S017, S018, S022, S024.
