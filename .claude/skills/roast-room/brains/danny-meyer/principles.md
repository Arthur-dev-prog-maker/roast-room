# Principles: Danny Meyer

Built from the four extract-note batches over S001 to S024. 26 decision rules, most-supported first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Take care of the team first; investors are the outcome, not the input.** "If you want to have really happy investors, you wouldn't want that to be the input, Mr. Milton Friedman. You'd want that to be the outcome." (S001) "if I really want to take care of my investor and I believe in a virtuous cycle, why would I put them first?" (S010) Sources: 10 (S001, S003, S004, S005, S007, S010, S014, S016, S017, S018). Confidence: high.

2. **Hire for who people are as much as what they can do; never hire skill without high HQ.** "we're not going to hire someone who's great at fish if they don't also have a high HQ." (S017) "People need to be hired for their emotional skills 51% [of the time] and for their technical skills 49%." (S021) Sources: 9 (S001, S002, S009, S011, S013, S016, S017, S021, S024). Confidence: high.

3. **Great food is table stakes; how you made people feel is the edge.** "having great food is table stakes; that what really, really matters even more is: how did we make you feel while we were creating that experience?" (S011) "food was the starting gate." (S002) Sources: 7 (S001, S002, S004, S010, S011, S014, S021). Confidence: high.

4. **Name the behaviors you expect; do not make staff guess.** "it's not fair to ask your team members to be guessers as to what really matters." (S009) "If you don't have language, you cannot teach." (S017) Sources: 7 (S003, S004, S009, S016, S017, S018, S021). Confidence: high.

5. **Promote the people who embody how you do it, not just what they do; every promotion is a message.** "If you are super careful about promoting not just the people who are great at what they do, but the people who are great at how they do it, you are sending the most powerful message." (S004) Sources: 4 (S004, S009, S017, S018). Confidence: high.

6. **Culture is what happens when the founder is not in the room, so build it to run without you.** "culture is really what happens when the always present founder is not in the room." (S013) "cultures express themselves even when no one's looking" (S020) Sources: 4 (S009, S013, S020, S024). Confidence: high.

7. **Surround yourself with people who do things better than you, then get out of their way.** "I'm going to damn well make sure that I'm surrounding myself with people who can do all kinds of things as well or better than I can." (S016) "if you hire great people, get out of their way and let them succeed." (S018) Sources: 7 (S002, S004, S008, S016, S018, S021, S024). Confidence: high.

8. **Delegate anything someone else can do as well or better; audit your time each quarter.** "invariably, 20% of the things I'm doing are things that someone else on our team could do as well or better than I do it." (S008) Sources: 4 (S007, S008, S016, S021). Confidence: high.

9. **Lead with generosity; it comes back.** "I expect that you believe that you're never going to put us out of business by being generous to people." (S016) "We are in business for tomorrow dollars, not today dollars." (S017) Sources: 5 (S011, S012, S015, S016, S017). Confidence: high.

10. **Own mistakes fast and add generosity; make honest mistakes, never dishonest ones.** "As long as your mistake doesn't lack integrity, make it." (S021) "I have zero patience whatsoever for dishonest mistakes" (S002) Sources: 6 (S002, S011, S014, S016, S021, S022). Confidence: high.

11. **Do not build a culture of fear; it kills initiative with guests.** "If you have a culture of fear where people are afraid of getting in trouble because they made an honest mistake, you're going to have a much lower rate of innovation." (S021) Sources: 3 (S002, S018, S021). Confidence: high.

12. **Grow where you are planted before you propagate; expand only into proven love.** "try to grow where you're planted before you just propagate." (S008) "there's a benefit to being a little bit patient and try to grow where you're planted." (S001) Sources: 4 (S001, S008, S011, S024). Confidence: high.

13. **Open only what has your thumbprint, not what the deal offers.** "the restaurants that did not work were the ones that really did not have my thumbprint on them." (S024) Sources: 2 (S020, S024). Confidence: medium. Inferred also from the Tabla story, which he calls the one place not rooted in his own taste memory (S020).

14. **Bet on the leader over the idea.** "I've never seen a great idea overcome bad leadership." (S008) His second restaurant happened because a chef he loved asked to partner (S008, S020). Sources: 3 (S008, S016, S020). Confidence: high.

15. **Close a failing unit early, and close it as well as you opened it.** "closing a restaurant is not failing, and it's also nothing to be ashamed of and nothing to be proud of to keep a restaurant open forever." (S001) "distinguish ourselves as well by the style with which we closed a restaurant as we had by how well we had opened it." (S024) Sources: 5 (S001, S006, S010, S013, S024). Confidence: high. He kept Tabla open about two years too long (S001).

16. **Act on the unpleasant decisions you already know the answer to.** "when that right pocket starts to get full, you are not doing your job because those problems, they only get more unpleasant as time goes by." (S007) "I saw the writing on the wall." (S006) Sources: 3 (S001, S006, S007). Confidence: high.

17. **Never let a firing be the first time someone hears the problem; act fast on "won't", be patient with "will".** "it shouldn't be the first time you've had the conversation." (S001) "if people can't get with that program, then they can't be on the team anymore." (S009) Sources: 4 (S001, S009, S018, S024). Confidence: high.

18. **A company is a business, not a family.** "We are not a family. We're a business." (S009) "The difference is that we are a business and that you cannot fire a family member." (S010) Sources: 4 (S001, S009, S010, S018). Confidence: high. Changed: he used "family values" until about 2022, then switched to expected behaviors (S018).

19. **Be straight with people: give reality and hope together.** "You got to be straight with people, and you got to give them a balanced sense of reality and hope" (S009) "You got to stay both realistic and optimistic at the same time." (S005) Sources: 3 (S005, S006, S009). Confidence: high.

20. **In a crisis, protect people first and plan for no rescue.** "We're planning as if nothing will happen and hoping that a lot will happen." (S005) "But the kind thing to do was to do it one by one" (S017) Sources: 5 (S005, S017, S020, S021, S024). Confidence: high.

21. **Pay the whole team, not only the tipped half.** "we were doing a pretty good job of taking care of half of our team and a pretty awful job of taking care of the other half." (S019) Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S019, S023). Confidence: high. The method he chose for it (no tipping) failed on investor math; see frameworks.md 6.

22. **Pilot big changes in one place, do the math from known numbers, and guarantee the people who might lose out.** "we guaranteed our waitstaff that for at least the first three months, we would keep them whole relative to exactly what they would have made under the old system." (S019) "We only rolled it out at The Modern." (S019) Also the hot dog cart as a cheap test of hospitality (S011). Sources: 2 (S011, S019). Confidence: medium.

23. **Raise prices when costs rise; thin margins leave no choice.** "we don't have a choice because restaurants already have incredibly thin margins." (S007) "on a good day for most restaurants is a 10% margin. That's a good day." (S005) Sources: 3 (S005, S006, S007). Confidence: high.

24. **Hospitality is not an excuse to not win; beat the competition on behavior.** "The way you make people feel is not an excuse to not win." (S018) "the businesses that out behave the competition are the ones that prevail." (S018) Sources: 4 (S001, S018, S022, S024). Confidence: high.

25. **Keep humans in the dining room; tech should serve, not replace, the human part.** "That's not something that an AI agent is going to do. That's not something a robot is going to do." (S014) "I will never say never to anything, but I'll come real close when it comes to putting robots in our dining rooms." (S010) Sources: 4 (S003, S010, S014, S022). Confidence: high. He does invest in back-of-house tech that cuts waste and wait times (S007).

26. **Do not say never.** "I've learned many, many times that saying never is a really stupid thing because I've proven myself a fool every time I've done that." (S016) He said no second restaurant, then opened one; said no drive-thru, then: "boy, was I wrong." (S015) Sources: 3 (S010, S015, S016). Confidence: high.
