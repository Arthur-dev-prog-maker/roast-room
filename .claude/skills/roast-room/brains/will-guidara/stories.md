# Stories: Will Guidara

Built from notes on 13 public sources (2022 to 2026), with quotes checked against the live pages.

16 stories he reuses, most-told first, with the lesson he draws. Source ids in parentheses.

1. **The street hot dog.** At EMP he overheard four European guests say the one thing they had not eaten in New York was a street hot dog. He ran to a cart, talked the chef into plating it in four pieces, and served it before the duck course. Lesson: listen for the unmet wish; personal beats polished; guests remember the gesture, not the menu. Later turned into a system (S001, S002, S004, S007, S014, S015).
2. **Last place on the 50 Best list.** EMP entered at 50th. It hurt, then it set the goal: be number one with unreasonable hospitality as the reason. Sources disagree on the year (2010 in S004 and S015, 2011 in S003). Lesson: choose your perspective and the kind of impact you are suited to make (S002, S003, S005, S015).
3. **Snow for the Spanish family.** Staff overheard that a family's kids had never seen snow. The Dreamweaver sent an SUV with sleds and hot chocolate to Central Park. Lesson: a dedicated role makes it easy to act on a good idea (S001, S007, S014, S017).
4. **A beach in the private dining room.** A couple's beach trip was cancelled, so staff filled a room with sand, beach chairs, and a kiddie pool. Lesson: people forget what they ate, never how they felt (S007, S014).
5. **His mother at the end of the block.** His mother, quadriplegic and unable to speak, waited in her wheelchair to greet him after school. Her smile taught him what feeling welcomed does to a person. Lesson: the root of his whole thesis, and of nonverbal hospitality (S003, S005, S011, S017, S018).
6. **Chewy flowers.** Chewy sends flowers when a customer's pet dies, later adding a painting. Lesson: plan responses to recurring moments in advance, and keep improving what already works (S017, S019).
7. **The pilot's cockpit tours.** During a tarmac delay a pilot gave cockpit tours. Lesson: a hospitable culture lifts everyone's experience, including people who never got the tour (S014, S017).
8. **The Dreamweaver role.** He added a floor job with no duties except helping staff carry out hospitality ideas, named for the Gary Wright song. Front-line staff originate ideas; the Dreamweaver executes them. Hiring one to invent all ideas fails. Lesson: fund the resource, not just the speech (S001, S002, S004, S007, S014).
9. **The real estate agent's yoga mat.** An agent who listens replaces the obligatory bubbly with a yoga mat and candle. Lesson: act on what you overheard, in any industry (S002, S007).
10. **The wedding song.** A server learned a couple had just married at City Hall, slowed their meal, and staged a first dance in the empty private room. Lesson: the best gestures can cost nothing (S004).
11. **The Budweiser cart.** For a guest whose late father loved Budweiser, the champagne cart came out carrying local Buds. Lesson: specificity over expense (S007).
12. **Cognac at the check.** NoMad left glasses and a bottle with the check so no one felt rushed and a big bill met a generous gesture. Most people poured one splash. Lesson: upgrade the most overlooked touchpoint; it rarely costs much (S017).
13. **Heated coats at EMP.** When a table's status turned green on the host screen, its coats moved to a heated room by the door. Lesson: build systems so the magic does not depend on one person (S019).
14. **The role swap.** Tense kitchen and dining room teams did each other's jobs for a while. Lesson: empathy is the shortcut, and there is no front and back of house (S003).
15. **Failed ideas: the rickshaw and the car service.** A rickshaw to NoMad was hot and awkward and failed. A car service idea took seven years before it worked, with a BMW 7 series in the Hamptons. Lesson: keep trying, and never shut down a new hire's idea just because it failed before (S017).
16. **The meditation app's renewal warning.** An app warned users before auto-renewal so they could cancel. Lesson: do right by the person even if it costs revenue (S019).
