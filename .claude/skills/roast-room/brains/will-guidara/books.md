Two books. Book text was not fetched; these summaries are in my own words, from what he and the sources say about the books.

## Unreasonable Hospitality: The Remarkable Power of Giving People More Than They Expect (2022)
- **Core thesis:** Excellence is only the baseline. The lasting edge in any business is how people feel, and that feeling can be systemized and taught. Leaders should give hospitality to their staff first and give them permission and resources to pass it on. He pitches it as a leadership book, not only a restaurant book (S005).
- **Key frameworks:** service versus hospitality (black and white versus color); one size fits one; the Dreamweaver role; the rule of 95/5 (S004); excellence as table stakes; make it cool to care and hospitality as a selfish pleasure (S011, S014); the best-offense-is-offense chapter on adversity (S018); the Christopher Russell rule of deciding for the restaurant as a whole, the role-swap meeting, and the worthy rival idea borrowed from Sinek (S003).
- **Context:** Published by Simon Sinek's imprint (S002), released in October 2022 (S018). He calls Danny Meyer's Setting the Table the old testament to his book's new testament (S016).
- **Taught in sources:** S001, S002, S003, S004, S005, S011, S014, S015, S018; named on the page of S007.

## Unreasonable Hospitality: The Field Guide (2026)
- **Core thesis:** The how to the first book's why: a workbook for putting the ideas into practice, which he wants to not feel like work (S017, S019).
- **Key frameworks:** the one size fits all, some, and one ladder; pattern recognition of recurring moments, deciding responses in advance and building systems to deliver them (S019); touchpoint mapping and the operating systems behind gestures (inferred from S017, S019).
- **Context:** Released April 2026 (S019).
- **Taught in sources:** S017, S019.
