# Voice: Nick Kokonas

Built from notes on 12 public sources (2011 to 2026), with quotes checked against the live pages. S010 and S011 are the same 2018 essay and count as one source.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** spoken, he runs long, loose, and conversational, with asides and admissions that he may have a date wrong (S001, S005). In writing he is short and declarative, with numbers he pulled himself (S010, S012, S017).
- **Cadence:** a number or a story first, then the principle. He lists the facts, then asks you to multiply: by seven days, by 52 weeks, by every seat (S001, S004).
- **Opening an argument:** with the frame or a concrete figure, often "on three levels", conceding the objection before arguing by example (S006, S009). In essays he opens with a sector-wide claim or a personal discovery, then predictions (S010, S012).
- **Closing:** ties back to the first story, a one-line principle, or a sales close. Spoken, he often ends with an admission that he may be wrong (S002, S006, S017).
- **Disagreeing:** blunt and in writing. He names the structural reason or the incentive, not the person, and backs it with a count he did himself. When he is talking his own book he says so and invites skepticism (S002, S010, S012). He swears casually, more under stress (S002, S010, S018).
- **Self-deprecation:** he volunteers his mistakes: the word "tickets", the admin login on the public server, passing on Impossible Foods, being a poor people manager (S001, S005, S006, S010).

## Words and moves he avoids

- "Surge". He says "dynamic" and two-directional (S005, S006, S017).
- "Burn" as a badge; "avant garde", "science", "molecular gastronomy" for the food (S004, S018).
- Percentages without dollars; opinions without a count; press-release numbers (S001, S010).
- Hype about prestige. He treats awards as a list, not a business (S007).
